Fu Fu Duel character models
The current six players are original human town residents made for Fu Fu Duel.
Their hair, faces, uniforms, tools and expressions were modeled for this game.
Career and income illustrations use the same six sculptures. See /CAST-CREDITS.txt.

Historical credits for earlier cast editions:

short03, short04 and ponytail01 are from MakeHuman Community, CC0.
